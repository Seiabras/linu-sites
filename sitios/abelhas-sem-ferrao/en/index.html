Brazil has more than 250 species of stingless bees. Do you know any?

They are native, don't sting and help pollinate fruit and flowers. Many people find a nest and destroy it without knowing. Shall we change that?

Do they sting?

No! Their sting is stunted.

Where do they live?

In tree hollows, walls and even poles, including in cities.

Can I keep them?

Yes, carefully and following the rules for stingless beekeeping.
