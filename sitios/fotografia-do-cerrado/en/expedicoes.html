Field rules

  • Keep your distance from the animals: no calling, cornering or feeding them.
  • No flash at night and no drones near nests.
  • Litter comes back with us.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a professional camera?

No. A phone with zoom already takes good photos of birds near the water, and the guide helps with light and framing. If you have a camera, bring a 300 mm lens or longer.

What should I bring?

Neutral-coloured clothes, a hat, sun cream, insect repellent, a water bottle and a red-light torch for late afternoon.

Can children come?

On the one-day trip to Brasília National Park, yes, from age 10 with a responsible adult. The longer expeditions are for ages 16 and up.

What if it rains?

In light rain the trip goes ahead: many animals show up right afterwards. In a storm, we rebook at no cost.
