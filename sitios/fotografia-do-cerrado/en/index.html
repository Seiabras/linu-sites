Some of the animals that turn up most on our expeditions.

Maned wolf

Chrysocyon brachyurus

The largest canid in South America. It hunts at night and also eats lots of fruit, such as the wolf apple, helping to spread the seeds.

Vulnerable (ICMBio)

Giant anteater

Myrmecophaga tridactyla

Uses its long tongue, up to 60 cm, to eat ants and termites. It has no teeth.

Blue-and-yellow macaw

Ara ararauna

Lives in pairs and nests in hollows of dead palm trees, such as the buriti.

Why the Cerrado

It is the most species-rich savanna in the world and, at the same time, one of the biomes that has lost the most native vegetation in Brazil.

2ndlargest biome in Brazil, after the Amazon
≈ 24%of Brazil's territory
3major river basins start here: the Amazon, the São Francisco and the Paraná
