Maned wolf

Chrysocyon brachyurus

The largest canid in South America. It hunts at night and also eats lots of fruit, such as the wolf apple, helping to spread the seeds.

Vulnerable (ICMBio)

Giant anteater

Myrmecophaga tridactyla

Uses its long tongue, up to 60 cm, to eat ants and termites. It has no teeth.

Vulnerable (ICMBio)

Blue-and-yellow macaw

Ara ararauna

Lives in pairs and nests in hollows of dead palm trees, such as the buriti.

Red-legged seriema

Cariama cristata

Its loud call, heard from far away at dawn, is one of the most typical sounds of the Cerrado.

Toco toucan

Ramphastos toco

The largest toucan. Despite its size, the bill is light and helps control body temperature.
