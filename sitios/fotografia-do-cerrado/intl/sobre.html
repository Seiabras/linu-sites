About

A wildlife photography company that wants people to get to know — and want to protect — the animals of the Cerrado.

Mission

To show nature to people, helping them connect with wildlife, so as to protect it and prevent its extinction.

Who it's for

Wildlife enthusiasts, photographers and students connected to nature.

The website's colors

The palette comes from the biome itself: the brown of tree trunks, dry grass and earth. Every color was tested with the WCAG contrast formula: dark colors carry white text and light ones, black text.
