Origin

We were born from a short circuit between two worldsBy day, the rift becomes an oven

In 2087, a barista named Aurora Vex spilled coffee on a warp reactor. The result wasn’t an explosion — it was an aroma.

The aroma opened a rift. On the other side was a city just like ours, but with lights that thought and rain that remembered. That’s where we found the beans we serve today.

Café Aurora is the only meeting point between the two cities. Here, steam rises slowly and time agrees to pause for a few minutes — as long as you order a second cup.

Every morning, when our sun and Ω-11's rise together, the rift that Aurora Vex opened with coffee shrinks until it is the size of an oven door.

The heat that leaks through from the other side isn't wasted: it bakes bread. The first batch comes out at six, and the smell crosses both cities before any alarm clock goes off.

Aurora Bakery is the same place as the café, in daylight. The night baristas become bakers, the counter becomes a shop window and the neon rests until sunset.
